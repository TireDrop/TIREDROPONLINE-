# TireDrop app plan (2026-10-20)

Goal (Justin): one website that works on desktop and mobile, plus a customer app on phones with live shipping tracking and easy use. Same codebase, built in stages, never a rewrite.

## What already exists (audited on main b4cd5a6)
- `public/site.webmanifest` (standalone, icons 192/512 incl. maskable, theme #070E1A), `public/brand/apple-touch-icon.png`, the viewport and theme meta. **No service worker, no offline page, no install prompt, no app-mode layout.**
- `/track` (TrackOrderPage, `POST /api/track`): order number or TD- reference plus the order email gives order name, date, payment and fulfilment status, items, tracking numbers with a carrier link, and where the tires are with the supplier. No prices, address, phone or delivery dates by design. Rate limited per IP; "not found" is identical for every miss.
- Telegram customer tracking bot (message an order number and email).
- Mobile call bar, chat bubble (hidden until the AI key is set), compare tray, Tire Size Finder camera scan, local delivery geolocation, GA4 events.
- Hard limits: Vercel Hobby 12 functions, all used (new endpoints ride on existing functions via `vercel.json` rewrites); checkout is request-only; house rules in CLAUDE.md.

## Stage 1 (this build, preview branch `preview/app-pwa`)
1. **Installable web app (PWA):** full manifest with shortcuts, real icons, a safe service worker (precache the shell, never `/api`, checkout, cart, track or schedule), branded offline page with the shop phone, update flow, install prompt.
2. **App mode:** when launched from the home screen the site switches to an app layout: bottom tab bar (Shop, Size, My Orders, Call, More), safe-area insets, no browser-style chrome. The browser site on desktop and mobile is unchanged.
3. **My Orders with live-feel tracking:** remember orders on the device (order number and email stay on the phone, with a "Forget" control and a Privacy page line), a status timeline (ordered, paid, with the supplier, shipped, delivered), automatic refresh while the app is open, a carrier tracking link, and a one-tap call to the shop.

## What "live tracking" can and cannot be today
- Shopify gives order status and the carrier's tracking number and link. The app can refresh that automatically and show a clear timeline.
- **True carrier scans** (out for delivery, delivered at 2:14 pm) need a carrier or tracking-aggregator API (FedEx/UPS direct, or a service such as AfterShip, Shippo or EasyPost) with credentials and a monthly cost. That is a Justin decision, listed below. It would ride on `/api/track` (no new function file). No delivery dates are ever promised by the site (house rule); scan events are the carrier's facts, shown as such.

## Stage 2 (after Justin approves Stage 1): App Store and Google Play
Capacitor wrapper of the same build, native camera scan, tap-to-call, share, optional push notifications for order updates (opt-in, designed to ride an existing function). Needs Apple Developer ($99/yr) and Google Play ($25 once), store listings, privacy labels. Written as copy-paste Chrome prompts for Justin.
