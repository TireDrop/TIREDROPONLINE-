# TireDrop homepage: conversion and usability audit (visual/measured)

Method: built `dist/` served with `vite preview` (port auto-moved to 4622), Playwright Chromium, 390x844 (touch, DPR 2) and 1440x900. `/api/status` mocked as live with chat on (so the chat button shows); other `/api/**` 404; third-party hosts blocked (GA4 blocked, ignored). Catalog status was `atd: sample`, so product cards and prices are sample data. Screenshots and raw data: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-cannavibe/d34449fb-614b-55e4-9ef0-ecf19b08a954/scratchpad/audit-shots/` (`m-fold.png`, `d-fold.png`, `m-sec-*.png`, `d-sec-*.png`, `m-hero-rest.png`, `m-shop-cards.png`, `m-search-*.png`, `m-menu.png`, `data.json`). Scripts: `audit.cjs`, `a2.cjs`, `a3.cjs`, `a4.cjs` in the scratchpad. No repo files edited.

Perf numbers are LOCAL, uncompressed, unthrottled, so they show page weight and structure, not real-network speed.

## Verdict

Desktop is good. The first screen says what is sold (headline), where it ships (top bar line "Shipping across the 48 contiguous states and DC" and the hero lede), and what to do (the "Find your fit" card with year/make/model, plus a clear Shop Tires button). Mobile is weaker. The first 844px never states that shipping covers 48 states + DC; the only shipping line there says "Free ship-to-store in South Florida", which reads as local-only. The hero form's main button ("Find Tires") sits about 1.7 screens down, and the first priced tire is about 3.8 screens down (y=3212). The page is 17.7 screens long on a phone. The performance, honesty and house-rule picture is strong: no invented reviews, ratings, "since" years, discounts or delivery dates were found in the homepage text. The main fixes are mobile-first-screen copy, shorter mobile hero, and shortening or reordering the long South-Florida-only block for the majority of visitors who are outside it.

## What works well

- Phone number visible from the first pixel on both sizes (top bar), plus a persistent two-button Shop Tires / Call bar on mobile, each about 46px tall.
- Desktop above the fold answers the 5-second test: headline, "48 contiguous states and DC", search-by-vehicle card with Find Tires, scan option, and a quiz link all fit in 900px.
- Search is fast and useful: typing `225/45R17` instantly offers "Shop 225/45R17 tires" and a matching product, with a search keyboard key and clear button.
- Mobile tap targets are good: 25 of 27 measured controls in the first 3 screens are at least 44px (the 2 exceptions are the off-screen skip link and the 36px top phone link).
- Honest proof: "TireDrop ... is new, so its own reviews are still being collected; the shop's are not. We do not reprint star counts here" with links to Google and Yelp. Address, hours and email are in the footer. No invented reviews, ratings, "since" years, discounts, coupons, delivery dates, APR or lender names were found in the homepage text. Free shipping is stated as 48 contiguous states + DC and installation as South Florida only, consistent with CLAUDE.md.
- Price display is clear and complete (set of 4, per tire, install add-on, free shipping, "estimate at checkout" instead of a date).
- Fast and stable locally: LCP is text (no image blocking), CLS about 0, one logo image, two font files, no horizontal overflow at 390 (scrollWidth 390) or 1440.
- Mobile menu is clear, with Shop Tires and the phone number pinned at the bottom (`m-menu.png`).
- Service-area detail is concrete (Sunrise address, three counties, price-from lines with durations).

## Not checked / limits

- No real network throttling, no gzip/brotli sizes, no iOS Safari (V6 zoom is a known-behaviour inference).
- Sample catalog mode (`atd: sample`): product count, images and prices may differ in production.
- Desktop distance to first priced tire was not measured numerically (shop section starts at y=1192, product row is further inside that 1360px section).
- FAQ answers were not expanded and read.
- Reveal-on-scroll animations were mid-fade in some section screenshots (700ms wait); text layout was unaffected.
