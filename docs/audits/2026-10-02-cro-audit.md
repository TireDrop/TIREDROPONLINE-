# CRO audit, 2026-10-02

A conversion audit of tiredroponline.com, done by walking four buyer paths as
a shopper would. It is a report only: no site code changed.

**How it was measured**

- **Build:** main at `7ecb70f`, the same code as production, built with
  `npm run build` and served by `vite preview`.
- **Browser:** Chromium (Playwright) at 390 px (phone) and 1440 px (desktop),
  reduced motion on.
- **Same as live:** the API answered as it does on production today. ATD is
  in sample mode (the 20-tire catalog in `src/data/products.js`, see
  `docs/integrations/atd.md`) and checkout is request-only.
- **Mocked:** NHTSA vPIC, through `scripts/vpic-mock.mjs`.
- **Not reachable:** the live domain and GA4 are blocked from this sandbox,
  so no traffic or conversion numbers are quoted. Impact is ranked by
  reasoning, not data. Every fix names the GA4 event that will measure it.
- **Clicks and scroll:** counted by the script. Seconds are my estimate for a
  person on a phone and are labeled as estimates.
- **Screenshots:** in [cro-2026-10-02/](cro-2026-10-02/), 12 PNGs, each
  under 70 KB.

## Top 5 fixes, ranked by impact ÷ effort

Effort: XS is under an hour, S is half a day, M is one to two days. Every
suggested line of copy follows the house rules: no deals, no invented
reviews, no dates, no APR, shipping to the 48 states + DC, and installation
in three counties only.

### 1. Turn "0 tires in your size" into a quote request (impact: high · effort: S)

- **Page:** `/tires` with a vehicle or size the catalog does not stock. On
  production today that is almost every vehicle, because the sample catalog
  holds 20 tires.
- **Problem:** a 2019 Toyota Camry search shows "**0 tires** in 215/55R17 ·
  20 other sizes". The only next step is a phone number
  (`05-zero-in-size-390.png`). A shopper who won't call at that moment has
  nowhere to go, and the visit is lost without a trace. This box is the most
  common end of the main buying path until ATD goes live.
- **Change:** in `src/pages/shop/TiresPage.jsx` (the card near line 733),
  keep the phone button and add a short form below it.
  - **Fields:** Name, Mobile phone (required) and Email (optional).
  - **Hidden fields:** the size and the vehicle.
  - **Copy:** "Nothing here in 215/55R17 yet. We can order it. Leave your
    number and we'll call with a quote for that size." Button: "Get a quote".
    Confirmation: "Got it. We'll call you about 215/55R17."
  - **Wiring:** reuse `submitForm()` from `src/data/forms.js`. Add
    `"size-quote"` to the forms `api/forms.js` accepts, so the lead takes the
    same Shopify → Flow → info@ path as every other form.
- **Expected effect:** a share of these dead-end visits become phone leads
  for the shop to call back. Today they leave and are never counted.
- **Measure:**
  - `generate_lead` with `form_name: "size-quote"` (it fires automatically
    from `submitForm`).
  - The funnel `search` (search_type `vehicle` or `tire_size`) →
    `generate_lead{size-quote}`.
  - For the denominator, add a `search` parameter or a new
    `view_search_results` event carrying `results: 0`.

### 2. Home hero vehicle search goes straight to results, not the 5-question quiz (impact: high · effort: S)

- **Page:** `/`, the hero "Find your fit" card.
- **Problem:** the hero card is the home page's primary action. A shopper
  picks year, make and model (3 fields, 2 taps), presses **Find Tires**, and
  lands on `/find-my-tires` at "Question 1 of 5" with the same vehicle filled
  in again (`02-hero-search-lands-on-quiz-390.png`).
  - **Taps:** reaching the shortlist takes 5 more screens and at least 7 more
    taps. Step 4 blocks with "Pick at least one" until priorities are chosen.
  - **Two paths:** the same vehicle entered in the `/tires` finder goes
    straight to results. The home page sends its most motivated visitors down
    the longest of the two.
  - **Checklist:** this is the open item "Hero finder goes straight to
    results".
- **Change:** in `src/pages/HomePage.jsx`, make `Hero` → `onSearch` navigate
  vehicles to `/tires?year=…&make=…&model=…`. That is the URL the `/tires`
  finder already writes, built with `serializeTiresQuery` from
  `src/lib/tiresUrl.js`.
  - **Unsized models:** the old reason for the quiz no longer holds. `/tires`
    now shows every tire with "Check fitment" plus the door-jamb prompt
    (covered by `check:remember`).
  - **Quiz link:** keep the quiz as a choice under the Scan button: "Not sure
    which tire? Answer 5 quick questions →" linking to `/find-my-tires`.
- **Expected effect:** the hero's vehicle path goes from at least 9 taps to
  2 taps from home to a list of tires, so more home visits reach a product.
- **Measure:**
  - The funnel, for sessions that land on `/`: `search{search_type:
    "vehicle"}` → `view_item` → `add_to_cart`.
  - Compare 4 weeks before and after the change.

### 3. Phone cart: put Checkout in the bottom bar (impact: medium-high · effort: XS)

- **Page:** `/cart` at phone width.
- **Problem:** the first screen of the cart shows the heading and the
  "Shopping for" vehicle box (`07-cart-fold-390.png`). No product, no total
  and no Checkout button are in view: **Checkout** sits 2,093 px down, about
  2.5 screens. The sticky bottom bar, the one action always in reach, says
  **Shop Tires** and sends the buyer away from the cart.
- **Change:** in `src/data/mobileBar.js`, add a `/cart` rule:
  `{ to: "/checkout", label: "Checkout", icon: "cart" }`.
  - **Empty cart:** `/checkout` with an empty cart already says "There's
    nothing to check out" and offers Shop Tires, so the rule needs no cart
    state and the prerendered HTML still matches the app.
  - **Test:** extend `src/data/mobileBar.test.mjs`.
  - **Follow-up:** on phones, show the line item and total above the vehicle
    box.
- **Expected effect:** more phone carts move on to checkout, with one tap
  from anywhere on the cart page.
- **Measure:** the `view_cart` → `begin_checkout` rate, device category =
  mobile.

### 4. Results start in the first screen when a search is active (impact: medium-high · effort: S)

- **Page:** `/tires` with a size or vehicle in the URL, at 390 and 1440.
- **Problem:** the first tire card is below the fold at both widths: 1,232 px
  down on a 900 px desktop screen and 1,216 px down on a phone
  (`03-results-fold-390.png`, `04-results-fold-1440.png`).
  - **What fills the screen:** a 370 px dark "Shop Tires" hero with a
    four-line lede, the breadcrumb, and the "Shopping for" box.
  - **The bottom bar:** on `/tires` it says **Find my size** and opens the
    5-question quiz, even after a size or vehicle is set.
- **Change:**
  - **Hero:** in `src/pages/shop/TiresPage.jsx`, when the URL carries a size
    or vehicle, render a compact header: one H1 line, such as "Tires for your
    2019 Toyota Camry" or "225/50R17 tires", with no lede and smaller
    padding. The vehicle box stays as it is. Keep the full hero for a bare
    `/tires`.
  - **Bottom bar:** in `src/data/mobileBar.js`, change the `/tires` action
    from `/find-my-tires` to `/cart`, labeled "Cart". Shoppers already find
    their size on this page.
- **Expected effect:** tires are in view on arrival, and fewer shoppers
  bounce off a page that looks like it found nothing.
- **Measure:**
  - The `search` → `view_item` rate within the session.
  - `add_to_cart` from `/tires`.
  - In GA4 Explore, scroll depth on `/tires`, which should drop because
    results arrive sooner.

### 5. Say what installation costs where "free install" is promised (impact: medium · effort: XS)

- **Pages:** every page header, the cart and the product page.
- **Problem:** the announcement bar on every page says **"Free ship-to-store
  & install in South Florida"**, but installation is not free.
  - **What the site says elsewhere:** it publishes "Installation from $25 per
    tire" (`/install`, and `src/data/services.js` `priceFrom: 25`). The cart
    toggle adds "+$25.00 each".
  - **Cart lede:** "Switch any line to free ship-to-store and we'll fit it
    for you" reads as a free fitting too.
  - **Result:** the buyer finds the charge in the cart after being told it
    was free. That is a trust break at the step where buyers are most likely
    to drop off, and it is a weak claim to publish.
- **Change:**
  - **Header:** in `src/components/layout/Header.jsx` (line 43), use "Free
    ship-to-store in South Florida · install from $25 per tire".
  - **Cart lede:** in `src/pages/shop/CartPage.jsx` (line 352), use "Local
    to South Florida? Ship any line free to our Sunrise shop and we'll fit it
    there, from $25 per tire."
  - **Both lines:** take the price from `getService("tire-installation")`
    rather than typing the number in.
- **Expected effect:** no price surprise in the cart, more install lines
  kept, and a claim the shop can stand behind.
- **Measure:**
  - `view_cart` → `begin_checkout` for carts that include an install line.
  - The share of `order_request` with `shipping_tier: "ship-to-store"`.
  - `remove_from_cart` right after the install toggle.

### Before any of these: measure phone calls (effort: XS)

The phone is the site's second checkout. It is in the top bar, the bottom
bar, every hero and the zero-results box, and the site sends no event when
it is tapped.

- **Change:** add `trackEvent("phone_click", { link_location })` on every
  `tel:` link, for example with one delegated click listener in
  `src/App.jsx`. Add `link_location` to `EVENT_KEYS` in
  `src/lib/analytics.js`, with values such as `header`, `bottom-bar`,
  `zero-results` and `checkout`.
- **Key event:** mark `phone_click` as a Key event, alongside prompt 25.
- **Why first:** without it, fixes 1 and 4 could look like losses when they
  only move calls into forms, or the other way round.

- **Fastest path:** from home to "Place Order Request" with an in-stock size
  takes about 10 taps and 10 fields, about 2–3 minutes on a phone
  (estimate).
- **Hero vehicle path:** the same trip through the hero vehicle search adds
  the quiz, about 1 more minute (estimate).

**Confusing steps and friction**

- **Pickup preferences:** choosing ship-to-store at checkout makes "Preferred
  day" and "Preferred time window" **required**. The same screen says "The
  day and window are preferences, not a booking". Make both optional or
  default them to "Any day, any time". The error text also shows in the
  brand blue rather than an error color (`08-…`). Measure with the
  `add_shipping_info{ship-to-store}` → `order_request` rate.
- **Install charge:** ship-to-store chosen at checkout doesn't add the
  fitting. The summary shows "Installation at the shop —" unless the cart
  toggle was on. A buyer who picks "Free ship-to-store … where we can fit
  them" at checkout can reasonably think the fitting is booked. Add an
  inline "Fit them at the shop (from $25 per tire)" checkbox on that option.
  Effort: M, because it touches the order totals.
- **Delivery estimate:** cards and the product page say "the delivery
  estimate is shown at checkout" (`DELIVERY_NOTE` in `src/data/pricing.js`).
  Checkout shows no estimate; it says "The delivery estimate is confirmed
  before your order ships". Change the product line to match checkout, so the
  site doesn't break a promise in the last step.
- **Sales tax:** the checkout page's own summary says "Sales tax: Calculated
  at checkout". Use "Added to your invoice", or reuse the "Added when you
  pay" note that already exists at line 986.
- **Wrong-size cards:** the heading says "These are not 215/55R17, so they
  don't match", yet each card shows the soft amber "Check fitment" badge and
  an active **Add 4**. Label them "Not your size: 215/60R16" and make Add a
  secondary button. That lowers wrong-size orders and the call-backs they
  need. Effort: S.
- **Vehicle box on phones:** the "Shopping for" box takes the whole first
  screen on the product page (H1 at 988 px) and on the cart. On phones,
  collapse it to one line ("2019 Toyota Camry · 215/55R17 · Change").

**Missing trust signals**

These are true and published in `src/data/business.js`, but missing where
the money decision happens.

- **Product page:** the [Yelp profile](https://www.yelp.com/biz/extreme-tires-sunrise)
  and the Sunrise address are absent from the product page. The cart has a
  good "Powered by Extreme Tires. A real shop in Sunrise, FL" card; the
  product page and checkout do not.
- **Proposed line:** under Add to Cart and in the checkout sidebar, add "Sold
  and fitted by Extreme Tires, 7712 West Oakland Park Blvd, Sunrise ·
  (954) 773-1896 · See us on Yelp · Google Maps".
- **Measure:** `view_item` → `add_to_cart`.

### Path 3: South Florida install seeker (`/install`, `/mobile-service/<city>`, `/schedule`)

**`/install`** (`10-install-hero-390.png`)

- **Hero CTAs:** **Book an install** (primary) and **Call**.
- **Out of order:** the page's own four steps start with "Pick ship to store
  at checkout", and booking is step 3. A visitor without tires is sent to
  book a fitting for tires they haven't bought.
- **Change:** make **Shop tires** the primary button. Make "Already ordered?
  Book your fitting" the secondary one. Effort: XS.
- **Measure:** `/install` → `search` / `add_to_cart` with `shipping_tier
  ship-to-store`.

**`/mobile-service/sunrise-fl`** (`11-city-zip-check-390.png`)

- **What works:** a clear H1, Book Mobile Install and Call in the first
  screen, and the bottom bar set to **Book install**. The ZIP checker is
  excellent: "33351 is in the mobile area. The van can come to you."
- **The ZIP is lost:** its "Book a mobile install" link goes to
  `/schedule?service=tire-installation` without the ZIP, so `/schedule` asks
  for it again. Pass `&zip=33351` and prefill it.
- **No path to buy tires:** an in-area result offers only booking. Add "Shop
  tires and pick mobile install at checkout".
- **Weak out-of-area link:** an out-of-area result offers "How shipping
  works". Make it "Shop tires (ships free)".
- **Where:** `src/components/ui/ZipCheck.jsx`. Effort: XS–S.
- **Measure:** a new `zip_check` event with a `result: in|out` parameter
  (add the key to `EVENT_KEYS`), then `install_booking` / `generate_lead`
  `form_name: booking`.

**`/schedule`**

- **Steps:** 5 steps: Service → Vehicle → Location → Time → Contact, ending
  in "Request appointment".
- **Prefill:** `?service=tire-installation` preselects the service. A
  remembered vehicle filled the vehicle step in 3 of 5 runs. The 2 misses
  were probably the script leaving `/tires` before the vehicle was saved. Not
  confirmed as a site bug; worth one manual check.
- **Required inputs, nothing remembered:** 3 vehicle + 3 location + 2 time +
  3 contact = **11**. Reasonable for a booking.
- **Two more ideas:** the ZIP prefill above, and a time step that offers
  "First available" as a window.
- **Measure:** `generate_lead{form_name: "booking"}` per `/schedule` page
  view.

- **Problems:**

- **Change:**
  - **Hero:** put the size / vehicle finder (the existing `SearchPanel`) in

  - **Trust:** add a trust row: "Shipped by TireDrop, the online store of
    Extreme Tires, 7712 West Oakland Park Blvd, Sunrise, FL ·
    (954) 773-1896 · Returns & refunds". The last item links to
    `/terms#returns`.
  - **Announcement bar:** on `/tires-shipped/*`, show "Free shipping to the

  - **Effort:** S, in `src/pages/shipping/StateShippingPage.jsx` and
    `Header.jsx`.
- **Measure:** the funnel `/tires-shipped/*` page_view → `search` →

## What already converts well (keep it)

- **Header search:** the typeahead understands sizes ("Shop 225/50R17
  tires") and lands on filtered results in one step.
- **Remembered vehicle:** it carries into product, cart, checkout step 3 and
  `/schedule`, so fewer fields are typed twice.
- **Phone buy bar:** the product page's sticky price + **Add to cart** bar.
- **Honest checkout:** "Checkout sends an order request; nothing is charged
  online yet" and "we call to confirm fitment and payment" set clear
  expectations.
- **Honest reviews page:** "Reviews are coming … we will not fill this page
  with ones we wrote ourselves", with links to the real Yelp profile. Keep
  it; it builds trust.
- **Clear errors:** every required field says what to fix, on both checkout
  and `/schedule`.
- **No sideways scroll:** none at 390 or 1440 on any page walked.
