# Home page brand strip: how the order was chosen

The strip on the home page (`src/components/home/BrandStrip.jsx`, order in
`src/data/brandStrip.js`) runs the brands best first. This is an **editorial
order, not an official ranking**: the site shows no numbers, stars or "#1"
labels. Research run 2026-10-17; re-check before relying on it.

## How much to trust it

Confidence 5 of 10 for the top ten, 3 of 10 for places 11-23.

- The main source, Consumer Reports' 2026 brand ranking, is paywalled. Most
  places come from articles quoting it, and those disagree in small ways.
- J.D. Power's 2026 study measures original-equipment fitment, not
  replacement tires, and publishes only the top three brands per segment. No
  current replacement-tire brand ranking was found.
- Places 11-23 rest on dated, vendor or blog sources. Brand parentage
  (Laufenn/Hankook, GT Radial/Giti, General/Continental) was not sourced.
- Move Dunlop lower if you want a pure quality order.
