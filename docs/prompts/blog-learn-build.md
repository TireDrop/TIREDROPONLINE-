   FAQPage and HowTo schema; sitemap lastmod; Lighthouse SEO ≥ 95.
1. **STOP for Justin's approval** of:
   - keyword research + keyword map

   plus a pilot of 5 Learn + 5 Blog. **STOP for approval.**

   - the rules check

   prompt, and monthly refreshes.

**House rules** (from CLAUDE.md), plus these for content:
- never "safe to drive", "safe" or safety guarantees
- every number sourced (NHTSA, USTMA, TIA, manufacturers, FL DHSMV)
- byline "TireDrop Team, Extreme Tires, Sunrise FL", with no personal names
  or invented credentials
- no near-duplicate articles (Google's scaled-content policy)
