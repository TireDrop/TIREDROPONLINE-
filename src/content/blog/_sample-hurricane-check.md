## Before the season starts

Check the pressure on all four tires and the spare against the placard on the
driver's door jamb, and look over the tread and sidewalls. If anything looks
wrong, have it inspected.

## If a storm is coming

Follow the [Florida Division of Emergency Management](https://www.floridadisaster.org/planprepare/)
for evacuation and preparation guidance.

## After the storm

Roads can be covered in debris. If you pick up a nail, read the
[tread guide](/learn/tread/sample-tread-depth) and call the shop.
